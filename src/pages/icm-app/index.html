ICM App

ICM App is TECAT's internal reporting tool. It reads TECAT's QuickBooks Online data to prepare cash forecasts, board reports and investor updates.

Connecting to QuickBooks

ICM App runs on TECAT computers and is not available to the public. A TECAT QuickBooks administrator connects or reconnects it from the computer where it is installed. Access is read-only.
