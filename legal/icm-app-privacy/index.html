ICM App Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

ICM App is an internal reporting tool operated by TECAT Performance Systems, LLC ("TECAT", "we"). It connects to TECAT's own QuickBooks Online company to prepare cash forecasts, board reports and investor updates. It is not offered to the public or to other QuickBooks users.

What we access

With authorization from a TECAT QuickBooks administrator, ICM App reads accounting data from TECAT's QuickBooks Online company through Intuit's API: accounts and balances, invoices, bills, customers, vendors, transactions and financial reports. ICM App does not create, change or delete anything in QuickBooks.

How we use it

Only to produce TECAT's internal financial reporting and analysis. We do not sell, rent or share the data for advertising or any other purpose.

Where it is stored

Data is processed and stored on TECAT-controlled computers. Access credentials are kept in the operating system's encrypted credential store. Copies of reports may be saved as files for record-keeping.

Service providers

Reporting may be prepared with the help of AI and cloud software providers acting on TECAT's behalf, under their business terms. They receive only what is needed for the task.

Retention and disconnecting

We keep report data as long as needed for TECAT's business records. A QuickBooks administrator can disconnect ICM App at any time from QuickBooks Online's app settings, which immediately ends its access.
