About TECAT

Wireless telemetry from Ann Arbor, Michigan

TECAT Performance Systems designs and manufactures wireless sensor systems that measure torque, strain, and temperature on rotating and moving parts.

Founder

TECAT Performance Systems was founded by Dr. Douglas Baker, an automotive industry veteran with many years of R&D experience in engine direct injection fuel systems, algorithms, firmware, simulation, and data acquisition systems and sensors. While designing engines at TECAT Engineering, which he founded in 1997, he needed a small, low-cost torque measurement system that would not harm the system under test, found none on the market, and invented his own wireless data acquisition system for rotating shafts. He established TECAT Performance Systems, LLC in 2014.

History

  1. 1997-2010

    T3 prototype VERIFY

    While designing engines at TECAT Engineering, the company he founded in 1997, Dr. Douglas Baker invented the T3, a non-invasive wireless torque measurement device for rotating shafts, and began building prototypes.

  2. 2010

    Model 462

    The Model 462 became the first commercially produced version of the wireless torque measurement system.

  3. 2014

    TECAT Performance Systems, LLC formed

    Dr. Baker established TECAT Performance Systems, LLC to structure the organization for future growth.

  4. 2015

    WISER 2000 and WISER 2030 VERIFY

    The WISER 2000 put an 8 g remote transceiver directly on the shaft, and the WISER 2030 launched in October 2015 with onboard data logging and triggering and remote flash.

  5. 2016

    WISER 4000

    TECAT began shipping the WISER 4000 wireless torque measuring and monitoring system in September 2016.

  6. 2018

    WISER 8000

    TECAT released the WISER 8000 Wireless Strain Measuring and Monitoring System in October 2018 at Automotive Testing Expo North America in Novi, Michigan.

  7. 2018-2020

    WISER OEM Platform

    The WISER OEM Platform brought wireless load sensing to oilfield pump jack load cells, followed by a 2019 patent for the Wireless Axial Load Cell and Sensor Assembly and a 2020 license agreement with Flintec, Inc. for its CC1W load cell.

  8. 2020

    Wireless load cell electronics ship for Flintec

    TECAT wireless load cell electronics, licensed to Flintec for its CC1W load cell, earned FCC and IC certification and began shipping to Flintec customers in April.

  9. 2020

    Three U.S. patents

    TECAT received U.S. patents for wireless pump control, drilling rig leg monitoring, and avionics performance indication.

  10. 2021

    IntelliTork with Pro-Dig USA

    The next-generation IntelliTork pile torque system, developed with Pro-Dig USA on TECAT wireless telemetry, launched and was in use on four continents by year end.

  11. 2021

    Technology licensing with Flintec

    Flintec began building TECAT wireless electronics into its load cells under a multi-year firmware license.

  12. 2022

    Pass-by-noise system for Cummins

    TECAT began developing a pass-by-noise measurement system for Cummins. It passed field trials in early 2023 and later became a standard TECAT offering.

  13. 2023

    Rod rotation sensing field tested

    TECAT and Flintec field tested wireless rod rotation sensing for artificial lift wells with Marathon Oil in North Dakota.

  14. 2024

    High-resolution and CAN systems

    TECAT released a 16-bit high-resolution single-channel system and a CAN-output dual-channel system, and the first production rod-rotation load cells shipped through Flintec.

  15. 2025

    Racing teams and next-generation load cells

    NHRA and tractor pulling teams ordered TECAT telemetry after a rule change, and Flintec introduced its CC PRO wireless load cell built on second-generation TECAT electronics.

  16. 2026

    TECAT 468 family

    TECAT introduces the TECAT 468 Single Channel and Dual Channel at Automotive Testing Expo North America in October.

Who uses TECAT systems

Engineering and racing teams at these companies use TECAT systems: Rivian, CNH, Sandvik Mining, JATCO USA, Scout Motors, the US Navy, AB Motorsports, Lycoming Engines, Hartzell, Neapco, Dana, Ford Racing, Toyota, Magna Steyr, Nissan, Tesla, Yamaha, Daimler Truck, and GM.
