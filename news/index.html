2024

  • ProductNovember 2024

    TECAT releases high-resolution single-channel and CAN-output dual-channel systems

    The single-channel system adds a 16-bit A/D converter, and the dual-channel system adds a CAN interface for vehicle data networks.

  • PartnershipNovember 2024

    Second-generation wireless load cell electronics launch

    The second generation of TECAT load cell electronics launched in late 2024, and Flintec introduced its next-generation CC PRO wireless load cell built on it in 2025.

  • Trade showOctober 2024

    TECAT introduces new products at Automotive Testing Expo North America

    TECAT exhibited in October and introduced its new high-resolution single-channel and CAN-output dual-channel systems.

  • CustomersOctober 2024

    IntelliTork with TECAT telemetry installs 30,000 helical piles at a solar farm

    Contractors used Pro-Dig USA's IntelliTork system, with TECAT wireless telemetry inside, to record installation torque on the solar farm's piles.

  • ProductSeptember 2024

    TECAT adds a pass-by-noise measurement system to its standard offering

    The system, first developed with Cummins, is now available to other vehicle test programs.

  • PartnershipJune 2024

    First production rod-rotation sensing load cells ship through Flintec

    The load cells add wireless rod rotation sensing for artificial lift wells. TECAT developed the capability with Flintec and Marathon Oil, which field tested it in North Dakota in 2023.

  • CapabilityMarch 2024

    TECAT demonstrates multi-channel landing gear temperature measurement

    A demonstration measured temperature wirelessly at several points on aircraft landing gear.
