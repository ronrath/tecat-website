Applications

Motorsports

Detect, Balance, and Tune

TECAT systems measure engine and driveline torque in real time on racing vehicles, including dragsters and pulling tractors and trucks, and send the data to a laptop or datalogger for analysis. The data rate is high enough to show what each cylinder is doing, and the system also measures vibration and acceleration.

Examples from the field

Per-cylinder torque at Kalitta Motorsports

Kalitta Motorsports uses a TECAT system to measure torque in real time and import the data into a notebook computer to analyze engine performance. The measurements run at data rates high enough to show what is happening in each individual cylinder.

Tractor and truck pulling with Corsa Instruments

Corsa Instruments, an Indiana maker of racing data acquisition systems, pairs a TECAT wireless torque system with its dataloggers for tractor and truck pulling. On a diesel pulling tractor, the remote unit rides on the driveshaft in a custom collar, and the base unit's analog output feeds the Corsa datalogger, including high-speed captures set off by a trigger. Corsa Instruments

Dragsters, experimental aircraft, and racing boats

TECAT systems apply to dragsters, experimental aircraft, and racing speed boats. On each, they measure torque along with vibration and acceleration.
