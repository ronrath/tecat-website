Applications

Energy

Wireless Sensors for Smarter Energy Applications

TECAT systems measure torque, acceleration, pressure, temperature, distance, and magnetization. Energy customers use them for down-hole drilling, pipeline pigging, and finding the depth of fault lines before perforating pipe.

Examples from the field

Hydraulic chain tong torque-turn

Down-hole drilling customers use a TECAT system to measure the force in the tightening arm of a hydraulic chain tong. It costs less than a typical torque-turn measurement system. A torque-turn application note is available.

Real-time weight-on-bit

A TECAT system measures torque at the interface between the drill bit and the soil. That gives drilling companies a real-time weight-on-bit measurement for better control.

Pipeline pigging

A TECAT system is part of a pigging system for older pipelines. A magnetometer small enough to pass through the pipe finds valve locations, and TECAT accelerometer data identifies weld seams. A pigging application note is available.

Fault line depth and casing compliance

Geological fault lines produce oil and gas more efficiently than other strata. TECAT offers a wireless way to find the depth of those fault lines so the pipe can be perforated at the best depths. Tracking torque signatures can also document crevices to help meet casing compliance regulations.
