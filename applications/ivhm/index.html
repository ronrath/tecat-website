Applications

IVHM

Redefining Vibration Testing

We are experiencing U-Joint failures in our fleet and they happen without warning.

What if we could measure temperature right on the U-Joint and use that information to warn the driver?

TECAT systems process data at the point of measurement for Integrated Vehicle Health Management (IVHM). They measure joint torque, temperature, and vibration and can send torque feedback to the engine ECU.

Examples from the field

U-joint failure warning

A fleet sees U-joint failures that happen without warning. A TECAT system can measure joint torque to predict failure, and it can measure temperature and vibration at the same time to find correlations.

Firing order and driveline vibration

TECAT data shows how engine firing order relates to driveline vibration. Each measurement is processed where it is taken.

Torque feedback to the engine ECU

A TECAT system can send torque feedback to the engine ECU. The torque can come from driveshafts, axles, or suspension components.
