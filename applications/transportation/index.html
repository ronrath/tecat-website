Applications

Transportation

Abundant Applications

TECAT systems started in the transportation industry and are installed in marine, automotive, and heavy duty applications. Installations cover engines, transmissions, and driveline parts, along with brakes, steering wheels, cooling fans, and trailer hitches.

Examples from the field

Installed across marine, automotive, and heavy duty

TECAT has systems installed at several locations in the marine, automotive, and heavy duty markets. The component list below shows where those installations sit.
